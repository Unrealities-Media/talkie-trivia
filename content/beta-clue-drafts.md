When Rango, a lost family pet, accidentally winds up in the gritty, gun-slinging town of Dirt, the less-than-courageous lizard suddenly finds he stands out. Welcomed as the last hope the town has been waiting for, new Sheriff Rango is forced to play his new role to the hilt.

**LEVEL_2**

A lost pet lizard arrives in a rough frontier town and is welcomed as its last hope. Appointed sheriff despite his lack of courage, he must try to live up to the role.

**LEVEL_3**

A lost household animal enters a dangerous community and is mistaken for the protector it needs. An unexpected position of authority demands courage the newcomer does not possess.

**LEVEL_4**

An outsider receives a role built on others’ hopes rather than proven ability. Living up to that confidence becomes unavoidable when the role carries real responsibility.

**LEVEL_5**

Being treated as everyone’s hope forces an unlikely newcomer to act the part.

## Enchanted (2007)

**LEVEL_1**

The beautiful princess Giselle is banished by an evil queen from her magical, musical animated land and finds herself in the gritty reality of the streets of modern-day Manhattan. Shocked by this strange new environment that doesn't operate on a "happily ever after" basis, Giselle is now adrift in a chaotic world badly in need of enchantment. But when Giselle begins to fall in love with a charmingly flawed divorce lawyer who has come to her aid - even though she is already promised to a perfect fairy tale prince back home - she has to wonder: Can a storybook view of romance survive in the real world?

**LEVEL_2**

An evil queen banishes an animated princess to modern Manhattan. Help from a divorced lawyer becomes a new romance, challenging the princess’s expectations despite the prince waiting in her storybook world.

**LEVEL_3**

A royal exile leaves a magical animated world for an unfamiliar modern city. Falling for a helpful local complicates an existing romantic promise and a storybook understanding of love.

**LEVEL_4**

Forced into an unfamiliar reality, someone encounters a relationship that challenges an idealized view of love and a commitment left behind.

**LEVEL_5**

A new reality challenges both an ideal of love and an earlier promise.

## The Mitchells vs. the Machines (2021)

**LEVEL_1**

A quirky, dysfunctional family's road trip is upended when they find themselves in the middle of the robot apocalypse and suddenly become humanity's unlikeliest last hope.

**LEVEL_2**

An eccentric family’s road trip is interrupted by a robot uprising, leaving the unlikely travelers as humanity’s last hope.

**LEVEL_3**

A family struggling with its own differences is caught in a worldwide machine uprising while traveling together. The group unexpectedly becomes the best hope of stopping the disaster.

**LEVEL_4**

An ordinary journey places a troubled group at the center of an extraordinary crisis. People who struggle together must now become everyone else’s hope.

**LEVEL_5**

A group’s troubled journey becomes a crisis that everyone needs them to solve.

## Babe (1995)

**LEVEL_1**

Babe is a little pig who doesn't quite know his place in the world. With a bunch of odd friends, like Ferdinand the duck who thinks he is a rooster and Fly the dog he calls mum, Babe realises that he has the makings to become the greatest sheep pig of all time, and Farmer Hogget knows it. With the help of the sheep dogs, Babe learns that a pig can be anything that he wants to be.

**LEVEL_2**

A young pig raised among unusual farm animals discovers a talent for working with sheep. With help from sheepdogs and a supportive farmer, he pursues a role nobody expects a pig to fill.

**LEVEL_3**

An uncertain farm animal finds an unexpected aptitude for another species’ work. Encouragement and training offer a chance to challenge assumptions about what it can become.

**LEVEL_4**

Someone unsure of their place discovers a talent outside the role assigned to them. Support from others makes an unlikely ambition possible.

**LEVEL_5**

An unexpected talent challenges the limits of an assigned place in the world.

## Rudy (1993)

**LEVEL_1**

Rudy grew up in a steel mill town where most people ended up working, but wanted to play football at Notre Dame instead. There were only a couple of problems. His grades were a little low, his athletic skills were poor, and he was only half the size of the other players. But he had the drive and the spirit of 5 people and has set his sights upon joining the team.

**LEVEL_2**

A young man from a steel town dreams of playing football at a prestigious university. Poor grades, limited athletic ability and small size stand in his way, but he remains determined to join the team.

**LEVEL_3**

An aspiring athlete pursues a place on an elite college team despite academic, physical and sporting disadvantages. Determination remains strong where qualifications fall short.

**LEVEL_4**

Someone pursues a highly competitive ambition despite lacking many of the expected advantages. Commitment must carry an effort that conventional measures would dismiss.

**LEVEL_5**

A persistent ambition survives repeated evidence of being an unlikely candidate.

## Hunt for the Wilderpeople (2016)

**LEVEL_1**

Ricky is a defiant young city kid who finds himself on the run with his cantankerous foster uncle in the wild New Zealand bush. A national manhunt ensues, and the two are forced to put aside their differences and work together to survive.

**LEVEL_2**

A rebellious city boy and his difficult foster uncle go on the run in the New Zealand wilderness. With a national manhunt underway, they must cooperate to survive.

**LEVEL_3**

A defiant young person and an unwilling foster relative become the subjects of a national manhunt. Survival in the wilderness requires them to set aside their differences.

**LEVEL_4**

Two incompatible companions become fugitives together. Remaining free and surviving depend on cooperation neither would normally choose.

**LEVEL_5**

A shared pursuit forces incompatible companions to rely on each other.

## Billy Elliot (2000)

**LEVEL_1**

County Durham, England, 1984. The miners' strike has started and the police have started coming up from Bethnal Green, starting a class war with the lower classes suffering. Caught in the middle of the conflict is 11-year old Billy Elliot, who, after leaving his boxing club for the day, stumbles upon a ballet class and finds out that he's naturally talented. He practices with his teacher Mrs. Wilkinson for an upcoming audition in Newcastle-upon Tyne for the royal Ballet school in London.

**LEVEL_2**

During a bitter miners’ strike, a boy leaves his boxing club and discovers a natural talent for ballet. A teacher helps him prepare for an audition at a prestigious dance school.

**LEVEL_3**

A young person discovers an unexpected talent for dance amid a community’s industrial conflict. Training with a supportive teacher leads toward an important school audition.

**LEVEL_4**

An accidental encounter reveals an unexpected aptitude during a period of community turmoil. A mentor helps turn that discovery into a chance at a different future.

**LEVEL_5**

An unexpected talent offers a new direction amid a community’s conflict.

## The Muppets (2011)

**LEVEL_1**

When Kermit the Frog and the Muppets learn that their beloved theater is slated for demolition, a sympathetic human, Gary, and his puppet brother, Walter, swoop in to help the gang put on a show and raise the $10 million they need to save the day.

**LEVEL_2**

A famous group of puppet performers learns that its old theater will be demolished. A human fan and his puppet brother help organize a show to raise the millions needed to save it.

**LEVEL_3**

Performers face losing their beloved theater to demolition. Two sympathetic relatives help arrange a fundraising show to protect the group’s home.

**LEVEL_4**

A group’s shared home is threatened with destruction. Supporters try to turn the group’s own talents into the money needed to preserve it.

**LEVEL_5**

Saving a shared home depends on turning talent into urgent support.

## Bend It Like Beckham (2002)

**LEVEL_1**

Jess Bhamra, the daughter of a strict Indian couple in London, is not permitted to play organized soccer, even though she is 18. When Jess is playing for fun one day, her impressive skills are seen by Jules Paxton, who then convinces Jess to play for her semi-pro team. Jess uses elaborate excuses to hide her matches from her family while also dealing with her romantic feelings for her coach, Joe.

**LEVEL_2**

A talented young woman joins a semi-professional soccer team against her strict parents’ wishes. She hides her matches behind elaborate excuses while developing feelings for her coach.

**LEVEL_3**

A gifted athlete secretly joins an organized team despite family opposition. Keeping the matches hidden becomes more complicated as feelings develop for the coach.

**LEVEL_4**

Pursuing a forbidden ambition requires an increasingly elaborate deception at home. An emotional attachment within the pursuit adds another complication.

**LEVEL_5**

A concealed ambition grows harder to manage when feelings become involved.

## Galaxy Quest (1999)

**LEVEL_1**

For four years, the courageous crew of the NSEA protector - "Commander Peter Quincy Taggart", "Lt. Tawny Madison and "Dr.Lazarus" - set off on a thrilling and often dangerous mission in space...and then their series was cancelled! Now, twenty years later, aliens under attack have mistaken the Galaxy Quest television transmissions for "historical documents" and beam up the crew of has-been actors to save the universe. With no script, no director and no clue, the actors must turn in the performances of their lives.

**LEVEL_2**

Aliens mistake an old science-fiction television series for historical fact and recruit its former actors to rescue them. The washed-up cast must handle a real space crisis without a script.

**LEVEL_3**

Performers from a long-cancelled space adventure are recruited by aliens who believe their fictional exploits were real. The cast must turn imagined expertise into a response to genuine danger.

**LEVEL_4**

A group is asked to solve a real crisis because others mistake its past performances for authentic achievements. Pretending to be capable is no longer enough.

**LEVEL_5**

Being mistaken for former heroes creates a real obligation to become them.

**LEVEL_2**

A boy discovers an old pirate treasure map in his father’s attic. He and his friends follow it in the hope of finding enough wealth to save their homes from demolition.

**LEVEL_3**

A group of young friends follows a discovered treasure map, hoping a hidden pirate fortune can prevent the destruction of their homes.

**LEVEL_4**

A threatened group turns a chance discovery into a risky search for a way to protect what they stand to lose.

**LEVEL_5**

A chance discovery offers a desperate way to save a shared home.
